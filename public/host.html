True stories. Surprising friends.

How to play

3–8 players each write 3–5 completely true stories about the theme.

Up to 10 stories are chosen at random. Guess who wrote each one. Every player votes, including the author, who casts a bluff vote. Nobody can vote for themselves.

A correct guess earns 2 points. The author earns 1 point for each other player fooled by the story. Bluff votes keep the round moving but do not affect the score.

Stories 6–10 double all points. After each reveal, let the author tell the full story before the host continues.
