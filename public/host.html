📖 STORY WARS Connecting…

100% TRUE EDITION · HOST SCREEN

True stories. Surprising friends.

How to play

3–8 players each write 3–5 completely true stories about the theme.

Up to 10 stories are chosen at random. Guess who wrote each one. Authors sit out their own story.

A correct guess earns 2 points. Authors earn 1 point per incorrect vote. No vote earns no points for either player.

Stories 6–10 double all points. After each reveal, let the author tell the full story before the host continues.
